1. What MINTA is

The contracts that do the work are ordinary contracts on VYRE, deployed by the network’s operator, which also holds the owner role in those that have one: anyone can read them, and see who owns them, on the explorer. MINTA is one app that uses them; other apps and launchpads can use the same contracts.
