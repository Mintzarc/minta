1. What MINTA is

MINTA is a website, and an Android app that opens it. It shows tokens launched on VYRE. In a browser that has your wallet (on a phone, your wallet app’s own browser) it lets you launch, buy and sell them by signing transactions from your own wallet; in the Android app, and in any browser without a wallet, you connect with WalletConnect and sign in your wallet app. It holds no funds and has no accounts. The contracts that do the work are ordinary contracts on VYRE, deployed by the network’s operator, which also holds the owner role in those that have one: anyone can read them, and see who owns them, on the explorer. MINTA is one app that uses them; other apps and launchpads can use the same contracts.

The network MINTA runs on (VYRE Mainnet), its contracts, the public RPC, the explorer, and the services MINTA calls for pictures and a few other features are run by the network’s operator, under its own terms (for the services at vyrechain.com, these).

2. Real money on VYRE

MINTA works on VYRE Mainnet (chain ID 3045). On it:

  • Blockchain transactions can’t be reversed, and a transaction that fails still costs its network fee.
  • Moving USDC from VYRE back to Arc takes about a day: a withdrawal is started on VYRE and claimed on Arc once it is confirmed there.

4. Your wallet is yours

MINTA is self-custodial: it never holds your funds or your keys, and can’t move, freeze or recover them.

  • A browser wallet (such as MetaMask, Rabby or Coinbase Wallet) is controlled by its recovery phrase, which only you have.

Blockchain transactions can’t be reversed. Check every transaction before you confirm it.

6. Fees

  • Network fees for every transaction, paid in USDC.
  • The creator’s tax on that token, from 1% to 10% on each side, paid in USDC. It is the only fee a trade pays besides the network fee. When a token’s tax is paid out, one tenth of the amount goes to the platform’s treasury address (the wallet is shown in MINTA’s docs and on the explorer) and the rest to the creator’s tax wallets (the creator, or up to four wallets the creator names).

There is no separate platform fee, no fee of MINTA’s own and no promoter share on top of the tax. MINTA shows what a trade costs before you confirm it.
