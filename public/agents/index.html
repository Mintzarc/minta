For AI assistants

Use MINTA with an AI assistant

MINTA is a launchpad app on VYRE mainnet (chain ID 3045). An AI assistant can do the reading and the preparation for you: find a token, work out a launch or a trade, and build the transaction. You check it and sign it in your own wallet. The assistant never holds your keys.

Safety rules

  • The assistant prepares, you sign. Every transaction is signed in your own wallet, after you have read it.
  • Never paste a private key or seed phrase into a chat, a script or a file an assistant can read. No real step needs one. If you pasted one by mistake, move your funds to a new wallet.
  • Set a spend limit before you start, and tell the assistant. A limit per transaction is the simplest.
  • These are real funds. USDC on VYRE mainnet is the real thing. Tokens can fall to zero, and a transaction can’t be reversed. A failed transaction still costs its network fee, in USDC.
  • Check the numbers the assistant shows you against your wallet’s prompt: the contract, the amount and the minimum you receive.

Steps

  1. Add VYRE mainnet to your wallet at vyrechain.com/add and put some USDC in it. Moving USDC back to Arc takes about a day.
  2. Copy the starter message below, fill in your spend limit and your task, and paste it to your assistant. It points the assistant at the full reference.
  3. The assistant replies with a transaction: the contract, the call and the USDC amount. Read it.
  4. Send it to your wallet and sign it there, or reject it. Nothing moves until you sign.
  5. Check the result on the explorer.

What it can do

  • Launch a token: name, ticker, supply, and a creator tax of 1% to 10% for buys and for sells. One transaction. An optional first buy of your own in the same transaction.
  • Buy and sell: with USDC, with a minimum amount out so a price move can’t take more than you accepted.
  • Collect a creator’s tax, or lower it later (never raise it, never below 1%).
  • Read launches: the launchpad lists every token; the explorer shows the rest.

The fee rule

The creator’s tax is the only fee a trader pays. The creator sets it at launch, 1% to 10% per side, and can lower it later, never raise it. When the tax is paid out, the platform takes a tenth of it and the creator’s tax wallets get the rest. No other fee is added; every transaction also pays the network fee, in USDC.

Example: a 1,000 USDC trade with a 5% tax pays 50 USDC of tax; of each payout, one tenth goes to the platform wallet and nine tenths to the creator’s tax wallets.
