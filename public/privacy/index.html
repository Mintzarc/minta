Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026. This policy covers MINTA, the website at this address and the Android app that opens it, which work on VYRE Mainnet.

The short version

  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. This site sets no cookies.
  • Blockchains are public: your wallet address and everything it does on VYRE can be seen by anyone, for good.
  • MINTA never holds your funds or your keys.
  • MINTA is a set of static pages with no server of its own. When it needs one (token figures, pictures, a check of the transactions you send on VYRE before you sign them, your balance and withdrawals on Arc) it calls services run by others (VYRE’s public RPC, the network’s explorer, a picture API, a transaction check, Arc’s public RPC), described below.

1. The blockchain is public

Wallet addresses, balances, transactions, tokens you create (name, ticker and the link to their picture and details), trades and what the contracts record about your launches are recorded on the VYRE network, and summaries of it are posted to the Arc network. This is visible to everyone, through the explorer and any other, and nobody can delete it.

2. What is collected, and why

When you…Who receives what
Open any pageThe host (Vercel) sees your IP address and browser as part of serving the files, under its own policy. The pages also read a short list of tokens MINTA’s team hides (blocklist.json) from this same site. The pages’ fonts are served from this site, so no font service sees you.
Browse tokens, trade, or read balancesThe network’s public RPC (rpc.vyrechain.com) receives your requests, including the wallet address whose balance is read, and your IP address, to limit how often each caller can use it. Its operator’s privacy policy says how long that is kept.
Open any page (the ticker along the top), the token list or a token’s pageYour browser asks the network’s explorer (explorer.vyrechain.com) for a token’s holder count and for the block at a given time, so the explorer receives the tokens shown and your IP address. Its operator’s privacy policy says what it keeps.
Create a token with a picture, description and linksThe picture you choose is shrunk and re-saved in your browser first (which drops anything but the image itself) and then sent to the picture service (api.vyrechain.com) together with the description and links you enter. They are public, and are kept so the token’s page can show them. Your IP address is used to limit how many pictures one caller can send.
Send a transaction on VYRE (a launch, a trade, an approval, a payment)Just before your wallet asks you to confirm, MINTA sends the transaction’s details (your wallet address, the address or contract it goes to, its call data and the USDC amount) to a check service (api.vyrechain.com). The service runs the transaction on the network without sending it, to see what it would do, and looks up the addresses it involves with a third-party security service (see section 4; it never asks about your own address, and sends no amounts or call data). It also uses your IP address to limit how often each caller can ask. The check is advisory (it never stops a transaction): MINTA waits less than a second for it, and if it is slow, busy or unavailable, or finds nothing, it goes on without saying anything. When it does find something, MINTA tells you before your wallet asks you to sign. It is only a hint and can miss things. Its operator’s privacy policy says what it keeps.
Open the Wallet page, or move USDC between VYRE and ArcArc’s public RPC (rpc.mainnet.arc.io) receives your requests, including your wallet address, to read your balance on Arc and, for a withdrawal, whether it has been claimed there, and your IP address. Deposits and withdrawals themselves are transactions you sign from your wallet, recorded on the blockchains as section 1 says.

MINTA doesn’t sell personal information and doesn’t use it for advertising. For how those services keep what they receive, see the privacy policy of the services at vyrechain.com.

3. What’s kept in your browser

MINTA keeps a few things in your browser’s storage, on your device, so it works across visits: which wallet you connected, transactions still going through, when its opening film last played, your display settings, and the tokens and creators you chose to hide. Clearing your browser’s site data clears it. None of it is a cookie.

4. Other companies

  • Circle: issues USDC (MINTA has no other dealings with Circle).
  • GoPlus (a public address-security service): the transaction check asks it about the addresses in a transaction you are about to sign, never your own address, amounts or call data. It receives those addresses and the check service’s server address, and its own privacy policy applies to what it keeps.
  • Vercel: hosts MINTA’s pages.
  • Arc’s public RPC (rpc.mainnet.arc.io): reads your balance and withdrawal status on Arc, receiving your wallet address (see section 2).
  • Trust Wallet: only if you press “Open in Trust Wallet” (shown in the Android app, which cannot connect wallets itself), your browser goes to link.trustwallet.com, which sees your IP address, and then opens MINTA’s address in that app.
  • Where a token’s creator chose: a token’s details (its description and links) load from the address its creator gave (for example IPFS), which sees your IP address. Its picture is shown only when it is kept by the picture service (api.vyrechain.com); a picture link to another site is never loaded.

5. Your choices

You can read MINTA without giving anything beyond what any website sees. Nobody can change or delete anything on the blockchain. Questions and requests: MINTA on Telegram or X (@MintaLaunchpad). Requests about data the network’s services hold go through VYRE’s privacy policy.

6. Deleting your data

MINTA has no accounts of its own, so there is no MINTA account to close. Two things exist:

  • On your device. What MINTA keeps in your browser (section 3) goes when you clear this site’s data. In Chrome: Settings, Site settings, All sites, minta.vyrechain.com, Delete data. The Android app opens this site in Chrome, so its data is Chrome’s: clear it there, not in the app’s own storage settings.
  • On the services run for MINTA. The picture, description and links you gave for a token are public and kept so its page can show them; the services also see the IP addresses of requests, to limit how often each caller can ask (section 2). To have a picture taken down, or to ask what is held, write to MINTA on Telegram or X (section 5).

Nothing on the blockchain can be deleted by anyone (section 1).

7. Children

MINTA is for people 18 and older and doesn’t knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes

This page is updated when what is collected changes; the date at the top shows the latest version. See also the Terms of Service.
