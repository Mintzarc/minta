Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026; updated October 2, 2026 (a check, below, of the transactions you send on VYRE from a browser wallet, before you sign them). This policy covers MINTA, the website at this address. It is a draft for the test network and will be reviewed and completed, with the operator’s name and a contact address, before MINTA offers anything but test money.

The short version

  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. This site sets no cookies.
  • Blockchains are public: your wallet address and everything it does on VYRE can be seen by anyone, for good.
  • MINTA never holds your funds or your keys.
  • MINTA is a set of static pages with no server of its own. When it needs one (pictures, card checkout, email sign-in, promoter codes, a check of the transactions you send on VYRE before you sign them) it calls services run by others (a blockchain RPC, an image-and-checkout API, a promoter lookup, a transaction check), described below.

1. The blockchain is public

Wallet addresses, balances, transactions, tokens you create (name, ticker and the link to their picture and details), trades and which promoter you named are recorded on the VYRE network, and summaries of it are posted to the Arc network. This is visible to everyone, through the explorer and any other, and nobody can delete it.

2. What is collected, and why

When you…Who receives what
Open any pageThe host (Vercel) sees your IP address and browser as part of serving the files, under its own policy. Google Fonts, which the pages load their fonts from, sees your IP address too.
Browse tokens, trade, or read balancesThe network’s public RPC (testnet-rpc.vyrechain.com) receives your requests, including the wallet address whose balance is read, and your IP address, to limit how often each caller can use it. Its operator’s privacy policy says how long that is kept.
Create a token with a picture, description and linksThe picture you choose is shrunk and re-saved in your browser first (which drops anything but the image itself) and then sent to the picture service (api.vyrechain.com) together with the description and links you enter. They are public, and not stored with anything that identifies you; your IP address is used only to limit how many pictures one caller can send an hour. They are kept so the token’s page can show them.
Buy USDC with a card or Apple PayThe checkout service (api.vyrechain.com) passes your wallet address, the amount you choose and your IP address to Transak to open its checkout; Transak then collects your payment and identity details itself.
Sign in with email (where offered)Circle handles your email address and sign-in; the sign-in service (api.vyrechain.com) passes them to Circle without storing them.
Send a transaction on VYRE (a launch, a trade, an approval, a payment)Just before your wallet asks you to confirm, MINTA sends the transaction’s details (your wallet address, the address or contract it goes to, its call data and the USDC amount) to a check service (api.vyrechain.com). The service runs the transaction on the network without sending it, to see what it would do, and looks up the addresses it involves with a third-party security service (see section 4; it never asks about your own address, and sends no amounts or call data). It also uses your IP address to limit how often each caller can ask. The check is advisory (it never stops a transaction): MINTA waits less than a second for it, and if it is slow, busy or unavailable, or finds nothing, it goes on without saying anything. When it does find something, MINTA tells you before your wallet asks you to sign. It is only a hint and can miss things. Its operator’s privacy policy says what it keeps.
Arrive by a promoter’s linkThe promoter’s code is looked up at the promoter service (testnet-rpc.vyrechain.com), which receives the code and your IP address.

MINTA doesn’t sell personal information and doesn’t use it for advertising. For how those services keep what they receive, see the privacy policy of the services at vyrechain.com.

3. What’s kept in your browser

MINTA keeps a few things in your browser’s storage, on your device only, so it works across visits: which wallet you connected, transactions still going through, the promoter who referred you, when its opening film last played, your display settings and, for email sign-in, your email address and Circle’s session. Signing out clears the sign-in; clearing your browser’s site data clears the rest. None of it is a cookie, and none of it is sent anywhere by MINTA itself.

4. Other companies

  • Transak: card and Apple Pay purchases of USDC, including its identity checks.
  • Circle: email sign-in, and moving USDC between networks.
  • GoPlus (a public address-security service): the transaction check asks it about the addresses in a transaction you are about to sign, never your own address, amounts or call data. It receives those addresses and the check service’s server address, and its own privacy policy applies to what it keeps.
  • Vercel: hosts MINTA’s pages.
  • Google Fonts: the pages’ fonts.
  • Blockchain services: for the features that move USDC between networks, MINTA reads balances from public nodes of other networks (Arc and test networks), sending your wallet address.
  • Where a token’s creator chose: a token’s picture and details load from the address its creator gave (for example IPFS), which sees your IP address.

5. Your choices

You can read MINTA without giving anything beyond what any website sees. Nobody can change or delete anything on the blockchain. A contact address for questions and requests will be published here with the operator’s name before MINTA is offered with real money; until then, requests about data those services hold go through VYRE’s privacy policy.

6. Children

MINTA is for people 18 and older and doesn’t knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes

This page is updated when what is collected changes; the date at the top shows the latest version. See also the Terms of Service.

MINTA runs on the VYRE test network: test USDC and test tokens have no value. USDC is issued by Circle; MINTA is not affiliated with or endorsed by Circle.
